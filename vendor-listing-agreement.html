Vendor Listing Agreement

Effective November 17, 2026

This agreement is distinct from the general Terms of Service: that one covers using the site as any visitor, while this one covers the added responsibilities of actually owning a listing — whether you created it or claimed it (see Listing & Claiming Your Business).

1. About this agreement​

This Vendor Listing Agreement applies when you claim, create or manage a business listing on The Roadside Emporium (the "Platform"), operated by Jason Corbett, an individual operating as a sole proprietor under the name The Roadside Emporium, based in Nova Scotia, Canada ("we", "us"). It adds to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which also apply to you. If anything here conflicts with the Terms of Service, this agreement governs your listing.

You accept this agreement by checking the box when you claim or create a listing.

Authority. By claiming a listing, you confirm that you own the business or are authorized to act for it, and that you can agree to these terms on its behalf. We may ask for reasonable proof, such as a business email, a photo of your stand or sign, or a phone call. If more than one person claims the same business, we may decide who manages it, or ask you to resolve it between you.

2. Your listing and your business​

Accuracy. You are responsible for what your listing says. Please keep your hours, season dates, products, location and contact details accurate, and update them when they change. Shoppers use the open-time filters to decide when to visit, so out-of-date hours cost them trips and cost you trust. If your listing sits unchanged for a long time, we may mark it as unverified or ask you to confirm it.

Your products and sales. You alone are responsible for the products you sell and every sale you make, including quality, safety, pricing, refunds and customer service. You agree to:

  • Sell only products you are legally allowed to sell
  • Hold any permits or licences your business needs, and follow food safety, labelling, weights and measures, and tax rules that apply to you
  • Not list illegal, recalled, counterfeit or unsafe products

We do not inspect, approve or certify any vendor or product.

Your content. You keep ownership of the names, photos, logos and descriptions you add. You give us a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display, resize, adapt and use them to operate and promote the Platform, including in social media posts and marketing that feature your business. You confirm you have the rights to everything you upload. This licence ends when you remove the content or your listing, except for copies already shared or in routine backups.

3. Free listings and paid features​

A basic listing is free. We may later offer optional paid features, such as promoted placement, subscriptions or tools for running your business. Nothing is charged unless you choose a paid feature and agree to its price.

If you sign up for a paid feature:

  • Price and taxes. You will see the price, billing period and any applicable taxes (HST) before you confirm.
  • Billing. Payments are handled by a third-party payment processor, which we will name when paid features launch. You authorize us to charge your chosen payment method.
  • Renewal. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel. We will give at least 30 days' notice before any price increase.
  • Cancellation. You can cancel any time in your account settings. Your paid feature stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds. Fees already paid are not refundable, except where required by law or where we end a paid feature early without cause, in which case we refund the unused portion.

If a payment fails, we may pause the paid feature. Your basic free listing continues.

4. Other terms​

No guaranteed results. We work to bring shoppers to local vendors, but we do not guarantee any number of views, visits or sales. We decide how listings are ordered, displayed and searched, and may change this at any time. Promoted placements will be labelled as promoted.

Removal and termination. You can unpublish your listing or close your account at any time. We may edit, hide, suspend or remove a listing if it breaks this agreement or the Terms of Service, if information appears false, if we receive a credible complaint, or for legal or safety reasons. Where reasonable, we will tell you why and give you a chance to fix it. After a listing is unclaimed or removed, we may keep showing basic public business information, such as name, location and products, unless you ask us to remove it. See Resolving a Claim Dispute for how a disputed claim is upheld or revoked.

Our relationship. You are an independent business. Nothing in this agreement makes you our employee, agent, partner or franchisee.

Liability. The disclaimers, limitation of liability and indemnity in our Terms of Service apply to your listing. You agree to indemnify us against claims arising from your products, your sales or the content you post.

Changes. We may update this agreement. We will notify you by email or in your account at least 30 days before significant changes take effect. Continuing to manage your listing after that means you accept them. We may transfer this agreement to a corporation we form to run the Platform or to a buyer or successor of the Platform, who will take our place under it.

Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of Nova Scotia and the applicable federal laws of Canada.
