Managing Your Listing

Once you own a listing (you created it, or your claim was approved — see Listing & Claiming Your Business), sign in and go to that vendor's detail page, then click Manage.

What you can edit​

  • Stand Name, Tagline, Logo, Description, Products
  • Website, Street Address, Phone Number, Social Links
  • Latitude/Longitude (or re-use Use My Location)
  • Days Open, Opening/Closing Time
  • Photos (up to 6)
  • Active / Inactive — uncheck this to hide your listing from everyone except you, without deleting it (handy for an off-season closure). While inactive, a small "Inactive — only visible to you" tag shows on your own view of it.

Changes save immediately when you click Save Changes.

Joining markets​

If there's an Upcoming Markets section on your manage page, it lists markets you can set up at. Click Join on any market you'll be attending, or I'll be there (already joined) to drop out.

📷 Screenshot: the Upcoming Markets section with Join / I'll be there buttons.

Your stats​

If you own the listing, a Your Stats panel shows how many times your listing has been viewed, how many times someone tapped Directions on it, and when it was last viewed — a basic read on how much visibility you're getting.

Media needed

📷 Screenshot: the Your Stats panel (Views, Directions, Last Viewed).

A note on inactive listings​

Marking a listing inactive only hides it from the public map/list/search — it doesn't delete anything, and you (and admins) can still see and re-activate it at any time from the same manage page.
