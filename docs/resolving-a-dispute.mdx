# Resolving a Claim Dispute

If someone else has successfully claimed a listing that's actually yours — or you believe a claim on it is otherwise incorrect — you can dispute it.

## Filing a dispute

1. Sign in, then open the claimed vendor's detail page.
2. Click **Dispute Claim**.
3. Fill in your name, email, and a short note on your connection to the stall (e.g. "I'm the owner — this stall has been in my family for years and I never authorized this claim").
4. Click **Submit Dispute**.

:::info Media needed
📷 **Screenshot:** the Dispute Claim button, and the dispute form (Name, Email, Connection note).
:::

While your dispute is under review, the listing stays with its current claimant — nothing changes until an admin decides.

:::info Media needed
📷 **Screenshot:** the "Your dispute has been submitted" confirmation message.
:::

## What happens next

An admin reviews the dispute and either:

- **Upholds** the existing claim — it stands, no change to ownership.
- **Revokes** it — the current owner is removed and the listing reopens to be claimed again (by you, if you then go through Listing & Claiming Your Business).

You'll be notified by email either way.
