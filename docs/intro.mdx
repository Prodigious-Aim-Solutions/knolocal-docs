# The Roadside Emporium Help Center

How to use The Roadside Emporium — finding vendors and markets, creating an account, listing your business, and managing it once it's yours.

## In this guide

- [Signing Up & Signing In](/signing-up) — create your account, confirm your email, and sign in or out.
- [Finding Vendors & Markets](/finding-vendors-and-markets) — browse the map and lists, search by product, filter by open status, save favorites, and get directions.
- [Listing & Claiming Your Business](/listing-and-claiming) — add a brand-new stand, or claim one that's already listed but not yet claimed.
- [Managing Your Listing](/managing-your-listing) — edit your listing's details, hours, and photos once you own it, and see who's viewing it.
- [Resolving a Claim Dispute](/resolving-a-dispute) — what to do if a claim on your business needs to be challenged.
