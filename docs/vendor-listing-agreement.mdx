# Vendor Listing Agreement

:::danger Draft placeholder — not the real Vendor Listing Agreement
This page is structural plumbing only. Nothing below has been written or
reviewed by The Roadside Emporium's business/legal side, and none of it is
binding or in effect. Real text is tracked in
[knolocal#285](https://github.com/Prodigious-Aim-Solutions/knolocal/issues/285)
and this repo's [#12](https://github.com/Prodigious-Aim-Solutions/knolocal-docs/issues/12) —
this page gets replaced once that's supplied, not added to.
:::

This agreement is distinct from the general [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service):
that one covers using the site as any visitor, while this one covers the
added responsibilities of actually owning a listing — whether you created
it or claimed it (see [Listing & Claiming Your Business](/listing-and-claiming)).

## What a real version of this page will need to cover

A starting outline for whoever drafts the actual agreement — not a legal
checklist, and not a substitute for real legal review:

- Accuracy of the information you post (hours, location, contact details)
- Responsibility for content you submit — photos, logo, description,
  website/address/phone/social links
- What happens when a claim on your listing is disputed, upheld, or
  revoked (see [Resolving a Claim Dispute](/resolving-a-dispute) for how
  that process works today)
- Consequences of marking a listing inactive vs. it being removed by an
  admin
- How this agreement can change, and how listing owners are notified
