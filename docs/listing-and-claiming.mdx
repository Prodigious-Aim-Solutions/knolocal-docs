# Listing & Claiming Your Business

You must be signed in for either of these (see [Signing Up & Signing In](/signing-up)).

## Adding a brand-new stand

If your business isn't on The Roadside Emporium at all yet:

1. Click **Add Business** in the header (desktop) or the + button (mobile).
2. Fill in the form: Stand Name, Tagline (a short one-line description shown on the map and in search results), Logo, Description, Products (comma-separated), Website, Street Address, Phone Number, Social Links, Latitude/Longitude (use **Use My Location** to fill these automatically), Days Open, Opening/Closing Time, and Photos.
3. Click **Add Stand**.

:::info Media needed
📷 **Screenshot:** the Add Business entry point (header button, and the + FAB on mobile), and the Add Your Stand form.
:::

Your new listing is active immediately — you're automatically its owner, so there's no separate approval step for a brand-new stand.

:::info Media needed
🎥 **Video (5–8s):** clicking Use My Location and watching Latitude/Longitude fill in.
:::

## Claiming an existing listing

If your business is already on The Roadside Emporium (someone else may have added it, or it came from an earlier data import) but nobody has claimed it yet:

1. Open the vendor's detail page and click **Claim Business**.
2. If you're not signed in yet, you'll be asked to sign in or create an account first.
3. Provide a **phone number or email** (so we can verify the claim — either one works, and you can give us both) and a short **note** describing your connection to the stall (e.g. "I'm the owner" or "I manage this stand for the Johnson family").
4. Submit. The listing shows **Claim Under Review** to you while it's pending — it stays visible to everyone else exactly as it was.
5. An admin reviews the claim and approves or denies it. You'll be notified by email either way.

:::info Media needed
📷 **Screenshot set:** the Claim Business button, the claim form (email, phone, and connection note), and the Claim Under Review screen.
:::

Once approved, you're the listing's owner and can edit it (see [Managing Your Listing](/managing-your-listing)) — look for the ✓ **Verified** badge that then appears next to your business's name.

:::info Media needed
📷 **Screenshot:** the ✓ Verified badge next to a business name.
:::
