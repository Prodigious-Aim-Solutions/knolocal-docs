## What a real version of this page will need to cover

A starting outline for whoever drafts the actual terms — not a legal
checklist, and not a substitute for real legal review:

- Who the terms apply to: site visitors, signed-up users, and vendors who
  list or claim a business
- Acceptable use of the site
- Account responsibilities (accuracy of information provided at sign-up)
- Vendor listing responsibilities — see the related but distinct
  [Vendor Listing Agreement](/vendor-listing-agreement), scoped to listing
  owners specifically
- Ownership of content vendors upload (photos, descriptions)
- Disclaimers and limitation of liability
- How these terms can change, and how users are notified
- Contact information
