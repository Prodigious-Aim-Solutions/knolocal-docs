Finding Vendors & Markets

No account needed for any of this.

The three views​

Along the bottom (mobile) or top (desktop) you'll find three tabs:

  • Map — vendors and markets as pins on an interactive map, color-coded by whether they're open (see below). Vendors show a store icon, markets a warehouse icon.
  • Vendors — the same vendors as a searchable list/table.
  • Markets — the same markets as a searchable list/table.

Searching and filtering​

  • Type into the search box to filter by product (e.g. "honey", "corn").
  • Use the open-status chips to narrow by hours: Open Now, Open Today, Open This Week, Hours Unknown, Upcoming (for a dated market that hasn't started yet), and Closed. Several can be on at once.
  • If you're signed in, two more pills appear: Saved (only your favorites) and Owned (only vendors you own or manage).

📷 Screenshot: the search box, status filter chips, and Saved/Owned pills.

What the pin colors mean​

  • Purple = upcoming (a dated market that hasn't started)
  • Green = open now
  • Blue = open today
  • Yellow = open this week
  • Gray = hours unknown
  • Red = closed
  • Slate = a dated market that has ended (shown for a short grace period, then disappears)

Dated markets​

Some markets run for a fixed date range rather than every week (a holiday market, a one-off event) instead of recurring hours. Their card shows the date range up front, and they're automatically marked Upcoming before they start and Ended for a little while after — so you'll still see a market you had a direct link to even after it wraps up.
