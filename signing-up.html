Signing Up & Signing In

You don't need an account to browse The Roadside Emporium — searching for vendors and markets, viewing details, and getting directions all work while signed out. You need an account to save favorites, claim or add a business, or manage a listing you own.

Creating an account​

  1. Click Sign Up in the top header (always visible, on both desktop and mobile).
  2. Enter your email address and choose a password.
  3. Click Create Account. A verification code is sent to your email.
  4. Enter the code on the Verify Email screen and click Verify.
  5. You're switched to the Sign In screen — sign in with the email and password you just created.

Signing in​

  1. Click Sign In in the header.
  2. Enter your email and password, then submit.

Once signed in, the header shows your email address and a Sign Out button in place of Sign In/Sign Up. On mobile, this moves into the ☰ menu instead of sitting in the header directly.

Signing out​

Click Sign Out next to your email (desktop), or open the ☰ menu and choose Sign Out (mobile).

Troubleshooting​

  • Didn't get a verification code? Check spam/junk. There's currently no in-app resend option, so if it never arrives, try signing up again with the same email.
  • Forgot your password? Not yet supported in-app — contact support (see Contact Us in the header or ☰ menu) for help.
