Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

1. Who we are​

The Roadside Emporium (the "Platform") is operated by Jason Corbett, an individual operating as a sole proprietor under the name The Roadside Emporium, based in Nova Scotia, Canada ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, apps, emails and related services, and should be read with our Terms of Service.

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Our Privacy Officer, Jason Corbett, is responsible for how we handle personal information and can be reached at contact@theroadsideemporium.com.

Most of the Platform can be used without an account and without giving us any personal information.

2. What we collect​

We collect only what we need to run the Platform.

Business contact information used only to communicate with someone in their business role is generally not covered by PIPEDA, but we protect it with the same care.

3. How we use and share information​

We use personal information only for the purposes listed above, or for new purposes we tell you about and, where required, ask your consent for. We do not sell personal information.

Business listing details are meant to be public and are shown to anyone who uses the Platform. Account details such as your login email and password are never shown publicly.

We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the Platform, such as web hosting, maps and directions, email delivery, analytics and payment processing. They may use it only to provide services to us. Contact us for a current list of providers.
  • Authorities, when required by law, or to protect the safety, rights or property of our users, the public or us.
  • A buyer or successor, such as a corporation we form to run the Platform, or a buyer if the Platform is sold or merged, who must continue to protect your information under this policy.

Storage outside Canada. Some of our service providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. While there, it may be accessible to authorities under that country's laws.

4. Cookies, location, retention and security​

Cookies. We use essential cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in and remember your settings, such as your open-time filters. We may also use analytics cookies to see how the Platform is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, though some features may stop working.

Location. We use your device's location only when you allow it and only while you are using location features. You can turn it off at any time in your device or browser settings. The map still works without it.

Retention. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Account information is deleted or anonymized within 90 days of closing your account. Consent records are kept for as long as needed to show we had consent, and at least 3 years after your last email from us.

Security. We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, encrypted passwords and limited access to personal information. No online service is completely secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by law.

5. Your choices and rights​

You can:

  • See your information. Ask what personal information we hold about you and how it has been used.
  • Correct it. Update your account or listing yourself, or ask us to fix it.
  • Withdraw consent. Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email, turn off location access, or close your account.
  • Have a listing removed. Owners of an unclaimed listing can ask us to correct or remove it.

To make a request, contact our Privacy Officer at contact@theroadsideemporium.com. We will respond within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first.

Children. The Platform is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy. We will post updates here with a new date. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify account holders before they take effect.

Complaints. If you have a concern, please contact us first and we will try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Contact. Jason Corbett, operating as The Roadside Emporium, contact@theroadsideemporium.com.
