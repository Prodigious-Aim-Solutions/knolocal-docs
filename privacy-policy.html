Privacy Policy

Draft placeholder — not the real Privacy Policy

This page is structural plumbing only. Nothing below has been written or reviewed by The Roadside Emporium's business/legal side, and none of it is binding or in effect. Real text is tracked in knolocal#283 and this repo's #11 — this page gets replaced once that's supplied, not added to.

Data sources a real version of this page will need to cover​

A starting outline for whoever drafts the actual policy — not a legal checklist, and not a substitute for real legal review. Pulled from what the app itself actually does today:

  • Account data — email and password, handled entirely by AWS Cognito via Amplify; this never touches the app's own backend at all.
  • Analytics — page/event tracking via react-ga4, plus a separate backend (vendorAnalytics) that counts, per vendor, how many times a listing was viewed and how many times someone tapped Directions on it (deduplicated per browser session).
  • Vendor-submitted data about a business (not about the end user browsing the site) — website, address, phone, social links, and whatever phone/email/note a claimant or disputant submits when claiming or disputing a listing.
  • Image uploads — vendor photos and logos, stored in S3 via a presigned-URL flow.

Still to decide​

  • Retention periods for each of the above.
  • Third-party sharing, if any (analytics providers, AWS itself).
  • How a user requests their data or its deletion.

This list is a starting point for the business/legal side, not a finished policy.
